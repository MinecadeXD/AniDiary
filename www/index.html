AniDiary Help Guide

Everything you need to know about using AniDiary.

🏠 Dashboard
See your Total Anime, Unwatched, Watching and Completed counts. Tap a stat card to open My Anime with that filter. Continue Watching shows anime you are currently watching.
📚 My Anime
Browse your complete library. Use All, Unwatched, Watching, Completed, Movie and Series filters. Movie means an anime with only movies; Series means it has at least one season, including season + movie combinations.
➕ Adding an Anime
Tap Add Anime and enter the available information such as name, poster, tags, rating and release status. Add seasons, movies and OVAs in the order you want them tracked.
🎬 Seasons, Movies & OVAs
Add seasons normally and optionally add parts. Movies and OVAs can be inserted between seasons. They keep their own watch status and order. OVAs appear in Anime Details and progress tracking, but are not a separate library category and do not appear on anime cards.
▶️ Watch Status
Unwatched means you have not started it, Watching means you are currently watching it, and Completed means you finished it. Season, movie and OVA statuses contribute to the anime's overall watch status.
📺 Release Status
Release status describes the anime itself, independently from your personal watch status. For example, you can be Completed while the anime is still Ongoing.
⭐ Rating
Set your personal rating for an anime. It is displayed on the anime card together with the watch and release statuses.
📝 Notes & Review
Edit your personal notes directly from the anime details page. Use them for thoughts, favourite moments, context or anything you want to remember.
📜 Journey Timeline
Add milestones such as Started New, Started Again, Completed Available Seasons and Completed Full Anime. Month and year default to the current date, while saved older logs keep their original date.
🔍 Anime Details
Open an anime to see its poster, statuses, rating, tags, seasons, movies, OVAs, parts, progress, notes and journey events.
💾 Backup & Restore
Export your complete AniDiary database as a JSON backup. Backups include anime, seasons, movies, OVAs, statuses, ratings, notes and journey logs. Import a backup to restore your data.
⚙️ Settings
Use Settings for backup/restore, the Help Guide, app information and data management. Be careful with Clear All Data because it permanently deletes your local AniDiary database.
💡 Quick Tips
A normal series can be Season 1 → Season 2 → Season 3. You can place a Movie or OVA between them, such as Season 1 → OVA → Movie → Season 2. Watch Status and Release Status are independent.
